Privacy Policy: Money Matters Uploader

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Money Matters Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Money Matters (@moneymattersfile) to upload videos to that channel.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos with their title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time.
  • YouTube read access (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel is signed in and check the status of uploaded videos.

What the app stores

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. A small log of each upload (video ID, title, scheduled time) is kept in the owner's local files. No data is stored on any server run by the app.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. It does not use data for advertising, and it does not collect data from any other YouTube user.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also ends access.
